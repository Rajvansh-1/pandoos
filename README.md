## 🎶 The Pandoos Experience

Pandoos is more than just a music player. It is a completely centralized, heavily stylized music ecosystem that follows you seamlessly across your desktop, browser, and mobile devices.

- **Vibe-Driven Curation**: Our dynamic mood engine curates exactly what you need—whether you're crushing a workout, locking in for deep work, or just kicking back.
- **Cross-Platform Sync**: Pause on your PC, resume exactly where you left off. Your playlists, liked songs, and listening queue sync instantly in real-time across Web and Desktop using our cloud backend. *(Note: Our top Panda Engineer is actively working on the Android app, coming soon!)*
- **Silky Smooth Aesthetics**: A handcrafted interface featuring adaptive dynamic coloring, glassmorphism, fluid micro-animations, and full-screen immersive modes built with our signature *Purple Panda* design language.
- **Immersive Mode**: Get lost in the music with a cinematic full-screen player that dynamically extracts and shifts colors based on your current album art.

## 📥 Download & Install

Experience the ultimate desktop client. Pandoos Desktop brings native media keys, background playback, and beautiful OS-level notifications right to your machine.

### 🪟 Windows
1. Go to the [Latest Release](https://github.com/Rajvansh-1/pandoos/releases/latest).
2. Download `Pandoos.Music.Setup.exe`.
3. Run the installer and let the music take over.

### 🍏 macOS
1. Go to the [Latest Release](https://github.com/Rajvansh-1/pandoos/releases/latest).
2. Download the `.dmg` file.
3. Open the file and drag **Pandoos Music** to your Applications folder.

### 🐧 Linux
1. Go to the [Latest Release](https://github.com/Rajvansh-1/pandoos/releases/latest).
2. Download either the `.AppImage` (runs instantly without installation) or the `.deb` file based on your distribution.

> **Note:** *If you receive a SmartScreen or Gatekeeper warning on your first launch, simply click "More info" and "Run anyway". Pandoos is completely safe, verified, and open-source.*

## 🛠️ Architecture & Tech Stack

Pandoos is engineered using a robust, modern technology stack to ensure blazing fast performance and real-time responsiveness.

- **Frontend Core**: React 19, TypeScript, and Vite.
- **Desktop Shell**: Electron 42.
- **Styling & Animation**: Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, and GSAP.
- **Backend Infrastructure**: Supabase (Postgres Database, Auth, and Realtime Subscriptions).
- **Audio Engines**: Howler.js (Web) and highly optimized native extractors (Desktop).
- **State Management**: Zustand (Client) and TanStack Query (Server State).
- **Caching Layer**: Upstash Redis and IndexedDB for instantaneous offline interactions.
